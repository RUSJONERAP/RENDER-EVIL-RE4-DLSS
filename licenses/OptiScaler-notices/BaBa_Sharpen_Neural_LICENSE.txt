Barbatos Neural Sharpening (BaBa_Sharpen_Neural.fx 2.1, 12-channel neural network 2.8), by Barbatos (Barbatos
Bachiko), is used by the DLSS 5 neural sharpen in nrcore/src/DlssNr_NeuralSharpen.cpp. The HLSL there is a port of
the ReShade shader published at https://github.com/BarbatosBachiko/Reshade-Shaders
(Shaders/BaBa_Sharpen_Neural.fx, Shaders/Includes/Sharpen_Neural/ModelAB_weights.fxh, and the BT.601 luma / YCbCr
helpers of Shaders/Includes/bb_colorspace.fxh; repository commit 1adf127d): Model A's weights unchanged, the same
layers, residual clamp and scale, and artifact suppression, adapted to compute shaders. The shader's header gives its
licence as MIT; the notice below is the MIT licence for it.
